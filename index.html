How It Works

👤 For Employees (Completely Free)

  1. Register with email & password
  2. Fill the comprehensive Employee Form (personal, education, experience, skills, location, government ID + referee)
  3. Your profile becomes searchable by verified employers worldwide
  4. When an employer unlocks your contact, you may receive messages in your dashboard

🏢 For Employers

  1. Register your company account
  2. Fill the Employer Requirements Form (job title, education, age range, gender preference, experience, location/proximity, skills…)
  3. Click Search – system shows matching employees (contacts hidden)
  4. Select candidates → proceed to Payment (Card or Bank Transfer)
  5. After Paystack automatically confirms your payment, full contact details are delivered to your Message Centre
